Reading it the second way is the one that matters in an audit. Nobody
asks 'what does lesson A1.2 map to'. They ask 'show me where human
oversight is addressed', and the answer has to be a list of things that
exist, not a paragraph saying it is covered.

Every label links to the published source, and those links are fetched in
CI - so a framework that renumbers its pages breaks the build rather than
quietly leaving a lesson pointing at a 404.
