DORA Art.11 asks this directly. It is the clause most AI procurement
cannot answer, and it was written years before anyone deployed an agent.
'ICT third-party risk' (DORA) already applies to us and is owned by third-party risk management.
   The agent fact that engages it: the model provider is an ICT third party
   Ask: extend the existing control, not create an AI policy.

'audit controls' (HIPAA) already applies to us and is owned by the security team.
   The agent fact that engages it: the acting identity is not currently recorded
   Ask: extend the existing control, not create an AI policy.

'access control' (PCI DSS) already applies to us and is owned by identity and access management.
   The agent fact that engages it: non-human identities have no recertification
   Ask: extend the existing control, not create an AI policy.

Compare with the alternative ask:
   'We need a new AI governance policy and a new committee.'
   → new owner, new process, new budget line, six months.
versus
   'Extend third-party risk to cover model providers.'
   → existing owner, existing process, next review cycle.
