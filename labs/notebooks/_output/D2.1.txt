'agent traces: prompts' is 23% of the volume and is read by 1 query,
which can wait hours. Priced hot it is the line that gets cut when the
bill arrives, and cutting it removes the only source D5.1 can replay a
run from. Tiered cold it survives at 1% of the hot cost.
