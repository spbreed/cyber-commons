The working-hours hypothesis is the one to stop on. It matched 14 runs
to find 2, because 12 of the matches are the overnight batch doing
exactly its job. Precision 0.14. That is a tune, not a finding, and
shipping it would add 12 alerts a night to a queue for 2 real events.

Note what recall is NOT saying here. No hypothesis reaches 1.00 and none
should: a hunt is one hypothesis about one behaviour, and these six
anomalies are not one behaviour. Recall against every anomaly in the corpus
is the wrong denominator - recall against the behaviour the hypothesis
describes is the right one, and that is why each hypothesis is scored alone.
