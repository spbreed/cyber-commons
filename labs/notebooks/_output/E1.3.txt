5/5 disagree.
The worst inversion: the small local model with deploy rights tiers LOW
on model capability and CRITICAL on what it can actually do.
ASK:
   What can it change without a human approving that specific action?
      → autonomy — the largest term
   What data can it read, and is any of it regulated or customer data?
      → consequence of a leak
   Can it act outside our boundary?
      → reach
   Is it registered, with a named owner?
      → governability — an unowned asset cannot be remediated

DO NOT tier on:
   Which model does it use?
   How many parameters?
   Is the vendor SOC 2 certified?
   (these matter for LIKELIHOOD and vendor risk — a separate, smaller term)

worked example — a new request:
   'an agent that can issue refunds up to £500, reads customer orders,'
   'runs internally, owned by payments-eng'
   tier: medium
