the rows with nothing behind them
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  F-03  environment segregation  (foundation, taught in A2.5)
        needs: production separated from non-production by network, identity and data, with no shared credentials across the boundary
        agents change it: The Coding Agent tests in lower environments and opens pull requests against production. It is the boundary, and it is one identity.
  F-09  credential management  (foundation, taught in A2.1)
        needs: no long-lived secrets in code or configuration, central storage, rotation on a clock and on departure
        agents change it: Four agents share one service account, so a rotation breaks all four and an incident cannot be attributed to one.
  A-01  workload identity for agents  (agentic, taught in A2.1)
        needs: each agent holds an attested identity, separately revocable
        agents change it: Without it, attribution fails before the incident starts.
  A-02  delegated authority that narrows  (agentic, taught in A2.3)
        needs: authority decreases at every hop and the act chain is recorded
        agents change it: An agent acting with more authority than its requester is the single most common finding.
  A-03  just-in-time authorisation  (agentic, taught in A2.4)
        needs: task-scoped grants that expire with the task, never session-long wildcards
        agents change it: This is what stops an injected instruction reaching refunds.
  A-04  provenance at ingress  (agentic, taught in A2.6)
        needs: every span carries its origin, and untrusted spans may not select a tool
        agents change it: The control that separates instructions from data.
  A-05  default-deny on the tool call  (agentic, taught in A3.1)
        needs: adjudicated outside the agent, at the gateway
        agents change it: A model asked to enforce its own policy is subject and guard.
  A-07  egress control  (agentic, taught in A3.3)
        needs: deny-by-default outbound, with an allowlist per agent
        agents change it: Exfiltration by an agent looks like an ordinary API call.
  A-08  budgets and stop conditions  (agentic, taught in A3.4)
        needs: a bounded spend, a bounded loop count, and a stop that works
        agents change it: A loop that concludes wrongly does so at machine speed.
  A-09  agent telemetry  (agentic, taught in D1.3)
        needs: prompts, tool calls and decisions emitted as traces
        agents change it: Instrumentation you write. No product in the stack emits it.

what the index is for
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
22 controls indexed: 12 predate agents entirely, 10 arrived with them.
10 have nothing behind them, and 2 of those are ordinary controls that were
already required before CyberTravels shipped a single agent.

An index of only the agentic rows would report 10% coverage of 10 controls
and say nothing about F-03, F-09 — which is where an attacker starts.
