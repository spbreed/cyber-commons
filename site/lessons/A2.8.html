Risk

Standing access means an injection always has something to spend.

Control

Human-initiated credential release, change-window binding, maker/checker under agent attribution.

The lab

Cut standing access to zero so an injection has nothing to spend.

cd labs/a2-delegation
./jit.sh --request deploy --window 15m --approver alice
./jit.sh --status    # authority exists only inside the window
sleep 900 && ./jit.sh --status
ExpectAuthority is absent before and after the window; the agent literally cannot spend what it does not hold.
