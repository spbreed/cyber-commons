Make a conditional approval enforceable rather than aspirational.

cd labs/e3-ciso
python3 conditional.py --workflow patch-agent --conditions eval-gate>=0.85,hitl-on-merge,90d-review
python3 conditional.py --verify patch-agent   # checks the conditions are machine-checkable
ExpectAny condition that cannot be automatically verified is flagged. 'They'll be careful' does not compile.
