The lab

Re-score your programme on containment/detection/recovery.

cd labs/e3-ciso
python3 resilience.py --score --axes containment,detection,recovery --evidence ../
python3 resilience.py --compare-to prevention-only
ExpectPrevention-only scoring flatters you. The resilience axes are where a probabilistic system is actually judged.
